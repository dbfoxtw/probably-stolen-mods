# 版本紀錄（Changelog）

## v1.0.0（2026-11-04）

對應遊戲版本：正式版 1.0（主選單左下角顯示 VERSION 100-REV6，也支援 beta 分支的 100-REV9）

- 第一個公開版本。用圖包的圖換掉顧客與劇情角色的立繪；通緝令海報的大頭照從圖包的 `wanted1`～`wanted9` 自動產生，或用圖包的整張 `wanted.png`；模組畫面啟用的圖包都生效，排上面的優先。
- 設定一個開關：圖包作者模式（預設關）。
- 遊戲官方格式的 mod，不需要 MelonLoader、不需要任何權限，不讀寫存檔。在 [Steam 工作坊](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3830171392)訂閱，或下載這個版本的壓縮檔；範例圖包兼範本另外是一個工作坊項目，Release 也附它的壓縮檔。
- 2026-10-30 起先以 Beta（0.9.0）在工作坊不公開給圖包作者試用。1.0.0 的圖包格式和 Beta 相同，照 Beta 做的圖包不用改。Beta 的「讓給其他模組」設定拿掉了：圖包是指以立繪框架為前置的圖包 mod，其他更換立繪的 mod 可能會有衝突。

**English**

Supported game version: full release 1.0 (the main menu shows VERSION 100-REV6; the 100-REV9 beta branch is also supported)

- First public release. Replaces the portraits of customers and story characters with pictures from portrait packs; the faces on the wanted poster are made automatically from a pack's `wanted1` … `wanted9`, or a pack can provide the whole poster as `wanted.png`. All packs active on the Mods screen are used, the higher one winning.
- One setting: Pack author mode (off by default).
- Uses the game's official mod format: no MelonLoader, no capabilities, and saves are never read or written. Subscribe on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3830171392) or download this release's zip; the example pack (also the template for new packs) is a separate Workshop item, and its zip is attached to the release too.
- A beta (0.9.0) was shared with pack makers as an unlisted Workshop item from October 30, 2026. The pack format in 1.0.0 is the same, so packs made for the beta keep working. The beta's Yield to other mods setting is gone: portrait packs are mods that list Portrait Framework as a prerequisite, and other mods that change portraits may conflict.
